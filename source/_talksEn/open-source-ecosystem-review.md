This material is an **academic critical-reading seminar**. It is neither a full original research article nor an independent public talk. It remains in the presentations/talks collection because its primary artifact is a slide deck delivered orally, while the page identifies it semantically as an academic seminar.

The presentation reviews _From proprietary to open source: Growing an open source ecosystem_ by Kilamo et al. (2012), focusing on the difference between publishing code, enabling participation, and sustaining a software ecosystem. It also distinguishes the paper's analytical contribution from conclusions that its empirical evidence does not establish.

The LaTeX source is versioned in the repository and the PDF is rebuilt automatically as part of the site publication process.
