title: Why this site exists
description: A small public space to connect my work, writing, talks and contributions to free software.
date: 2026-09-14
---

I have been working with software, especially PHP, Linux and free software, for more than two decades. Over time, part of that work became scattered across GitHub repositories, conference platforms, videos, community groups, academic work and company websites.

This site is an attempt to bring those pieces together in one place that I can maintain directly.

The goal is not to create a polished personal brand or reproduce information that already exists elsewhere. I want a public and versioned record of the work I consider relevant: articles, talks, projects, research, community activity and technical material.

The site itself is also part of that experiment. It is built as free software, with its source code, content and history available on GitHub. Changes go through the same kind of review and automated checks I expect from software projects.

English is the canonical language because much of the technical and free-software community I interact with is international. Portuguese translations will be published when they are useful, especially for content connected to Brazilian communities and organizations.

Over time I expect this space to connect talks with their slides, recordings and events; projects with their history and impact; and articles with the discussions and work that motivated them. The intention is simple: make the trajectory easier to inspect, reuse and verify without turning it into a static résumé.
