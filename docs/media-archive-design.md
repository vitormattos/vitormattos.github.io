# Media archive

The bilingual archive exposes the same curated records through a visual mosaic,
event albums and a city-level map. The default mosaic uses Masonry with
horizontal ordering; DOM order remains chronological for keyboard and screen
reader navigation. The year rail jumps to visible records, with a separate
year filter and thumbnail-density control. Filters and detail links are
shareable through the URL. The base records and source links are rendered by
Jigsaw and remain accessible without JavaScript.

Libraries installed with exact versions and locked transitives:
Masonry 4.2.2 (MIT), PhotoSwipe 5.4.4 (MIT) and Leaflet 1.9.4 (BSD-2-Clause).
PhotoSwipe enlarges locally available covers and photographs; a native dialog
shows provenance, contextual links and next/previous navigation. Leaflet
is loaded on demand and displays a local simplified Natural Earth country
outline; no tiles, geolocation or visitor tracking are needed. The city
coordinates are approximate public event city centers, not photographic GPS.

The initial catalog contains 18 documented public references. Historic
recordings may be unavailable; dates are left unknown rather than guessed.
Slide covers are explicitly marked as such. The external YouTube thumbnails
are only requested after the visitor chooses to load them. No players or
third-party images load by default. Linked-only photos remain references.
The FISL 2018 photograph is credited to Alexandre A. Kupac / FISL 18,
licensed CC BY 2.0, resized and converted to WebP with metadata stripped.
See its Wikimedia Commons file page for original credit and license.

Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 country data was obtained from
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector and reduced to South America
and country names. Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

Curated public media records live in data/media.php. Only add images with
documented reuse rights and credits; do not add private paths, file IDs,
biometrics, EXIF or exact capture coordinates. Use optimized local variants
and explicit dimensions for new photographs. Article relationships indicate
related subject matter, not attendance at an event.
