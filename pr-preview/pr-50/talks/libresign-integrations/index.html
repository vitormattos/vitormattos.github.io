About this presentation

This presentation introduces LibreSign through the broader context of free software, privacy and technological sovereignty. It covers the project's integration with Nextcloud, its signing architecture and API, the LibreSign integration with GLPI, and the technical and community challenges involved in maintaining an open source electronic-signature platform.

The original deck is in Brazilian Portuguese and is preserved on Slides.com.
