Christian communion in the face of digital ethics dilemmas

A biblical approach to managing sensitive data in the church. Theology bachelor's monograph submitted to Seminário Teológico Presbiteriano Rev. Ashbel Green Simonton in 2025.

By Vitor Mattos

Bachelor's monograph in Theology

Original title: A comunhão cristã frente aos dilemas da ética digital: uma abordagem bíblica sobre a gestão de dados sensíveis na igreja
Author: Vitor Mattos de Souza
Institution: Seminário Teológico Presbiteriano Rev. Ashbel Green Simonton
Degree: Bacharel em Teologia
Advisor: Rev. André Monteiro
Location: Rio de Janeiro
Year: 2025

This academic record presents the 2025 monograph in English while preserving the original Portuguese title. The work examines digital-ethics dilemmas in the context of Christian communion, focusing on the management of sensitive data in churches from a biblical perspective. It connects privacy, data security and technology use with ethical reflection in ecclesiastical communities.
